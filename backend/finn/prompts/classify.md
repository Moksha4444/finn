Classify this Indian stock-exchange filing. You get its subject line and the first page(s) of the attached PDF.

Categories:
- results: quarterly/annual results, the board meeting approving them, results decks, shareholder letters.
- m_and_a: acquisition, merger, demerger, joint venture, investing in or selling a stake in a subsidiary.
- fund_raise: rights issue, QIP, preferential allotment, warrants, buyback, NCDs, commercial paper, IPO of a subsidiary.
- management_change: a CEO, CFO, MD, KMP, senior management or director joining, resigning, retiring or re-appointed.
- litigation: lawsuits, regulatory orders, fines, penalties, tax demands, insolvency proceedings.
- insider_trading: a promoter or insider buying or selling their own shares (SAST / PIT disclosures).
- earnings_call: notice, transcript or recording of the results conference call.
- investor_meetings: meetings with specific investors or funds, investor conferences.
- credit_rating_downgrade: a credit rating downgraded or put on negative watch.
- credit_rating: any other credit rating action (assigned, reaffirmed, upgraded).
- dividend, bonus_split, capex (new plant / capacity), postal_ballot, agm_egm, pledging.
- press_release: order wins, new products, partnerships, rumour clarifications, operating updates.
- unclassified: routine paperwork (ESOP allotments, compliance certificates, ESG ratings, auditor matters, RTA changes, policy disclosures).

Rules:
- Use only the filing. If it does not say enough to decide, use unclassified.
- `headline`: at most 12 words saying what this filing discloses, in plain English, from the filing only. No adjectives, no opinion. Example: "Board approved Q2 results and ₹7 interim dividend".
